05 / THE WHISPERS

FAQ?

Do I need advanced coding skills?

No. Beginners are welcome. Pick a realistic problem, learn as you build and use mentor support.

Can I participate alone?

Yes. Solo builders can participate and find teammates during the opening session.

What should I bring?

Bring your laptop, charger, student ID and anything you need for a focused 24-hour build.

How are projects judged?

Judges consider problem relevance, originality, technical execution, usability, design and the final demo.
